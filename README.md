&#x20;🍔 Crave'd – Food Ordering Website



Crave'd is a modern and responsive food ordering website designed to make browsing food and managing orders simple and enjoyable.

&#x20;✨ Features



\- 🏠 Attractive home page

\- 🍔 Variety of food items

\- 🏷️ Food categories

\- 🛒 Add items to cart

\- ➕ Increase item quantity

\- ➖ Decrease item quantity

\- 💰 Automatic total price calculation

\- 🗑️ Clear cart option

\- 💳 Checkout button

\- 📱 Responsive design for different screen sizes

\- 🎨 Modern black and gold themed interface



&#x20;🍽️ Menu



The website includes a variety of items such as:



\- Smoky Brisket Burger

\- Classic Cheese Burger

\- Truffle Mushroom Pizza

\- Margherita Pizza

\- Honey Habanero Wings

\- Loaded Garlic Fries

\- Loaded Cheese Nachos

\- Chicken Shawarma

\- Paneer Shawarma

\- Creamy Alfredo Pasta

\- Sizzler Brownie

\- Brownie Sundae

\- Chocolate Milkshake
